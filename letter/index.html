Mail it right

  1. Print and sign each letter. Keep a copy.
  2. Include a copy of your driver's license or state ID and a utility bill or bank statement showing your address.
  3. Circle or highlight the disputed items on a copy of your report and include it.
  4. Send by USPS Certified Mail with Return Receipt so you can prove the date it was received.
  5. Mark the date. The bureau generally has 30 days to investigate (45 if you send more info during the investigation).
