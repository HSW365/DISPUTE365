Questions

Straight answers.

Can this remove anything from my report?

No one can legally remove accurate, current information. DISPUTE365 helps you challenge items that are inaccurate, incomplete, unverifiable or older than federal law allows. Bureaus must delete what can't be verified.

Where does my report go?

Nowhere. The PDF is read inside your browser. Only the item details you choose to dispute are sent to build your letters, and they are not stored.

Do you mail the letters for me?

No. You print and send them yourself, ideally by certified mail with return receipt. That keeps you in control and gives you proof of delivery.

How long does it take?

Bureaus generally have 30 days from receipt to finish a reinvestigation (45 if you send more information during the review). Most people run two or three rounds.

Which report file works best?

Text-based PDFs from annualcreditreport.com or the bureau websites. Image scans can't be read automatically; paste the text or add items by hand.

How do I cancel?

Card plans cancel from Billing in your account. CashApp and Zelle plans simply end when you stop paying. No contracts, no cancellation fees.
